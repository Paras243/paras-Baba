About Paras Baba: The Voice of Sanatan Dharma

A Devotee's Journey

Paras Baba is a revered spiritual teacher and **Katha Vachak** dedicated to spreading the profound wisdom of Sanatan Dharma. His powerful and heartfelt narration of sacred texts—from the deep philosophy of the **Bhagavad Gita** to the heroic narratives of the **Ramayana** and the divine insights of the **Puranas**—has become a beacon of hope and knowledge.

Rooted in deep devotion to Lord Shiva, he embodies the spirit of a true **Shiv Bhakt**. His mission transcends religious boundaries, focusing on unifying spiritual seekers and fostering the cultural renaissance of **Akhand Bharat** through truth, morality, and selfless service (*Seva*).

Shiv Bhakt Katha Vachak Namo Vasudevaye

The Three Pillars of Our Mission

Gyan: Spreading Vedic Knowledge

To make the deep spiritual science of the Vedas, Upanishads, and Granths accessible to the common person, bridging the gap between ancient wisdom and modern life.

Bhakti: Cultivating Pure Devotion

Fostering an environment of pure love and devotion to the Divine. Through *Bhajans* and *Kirtans*, we encourage a personal, heartfelt relationship with God.

Seva: Uplifting Humanity

Committing to selfless service in the community. Our spiritual awakening must translate into compassion and action for the welfare of all beings (*Sarve Bhavantu Sukhinah*).
